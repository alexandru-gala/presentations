04 · Behind the scenes

The tools had to grow up to keep up with you.

The uncomfortable middle
Capability came with instability.
  • 150+ agents, 170+ skills — built fast, in the open.
  • Fragile wrappers, flaky CLIs, duplicated patterns.
  • Every stall cost a session; every silent failure cost trust.
Since July
From “it exists” to “it survives production”.
  • 751 commits of hardening in shared-config alone.
  • Auth preflights, timeouts, retries, security scanning on every merge.
  • Weak paths retired. Strong paths made boring — and dependable.
We didn’t just add tools — we stabilized the ones that earned trust.

07 · Border 3 of 3

Technical support joins the whole product — not just the backend.

Historically
Backend was the boundary.
  • Support solved what lived in the services and the database.
  • Anything touching mobile or web meant a handoff — and a wait.
The goal
Full-stack problems, resolved end to end.
  • Backend expertise stays valuable — it stops being the border.
  • Escalation becomes a choice, not the default.
  • Same agents, same skills, same factories — now in support’s hands too.
One team owns the customer’s problem until it’s done — wherever it lives.
